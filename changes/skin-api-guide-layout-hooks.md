- Skin API 2: new guide window hooks for skins that lay the window out
  their own way: `guideTakes` (a goal the skin shows on a card of its own,
  out of the window's rows), `guidePerson` (the step's header, laid out and
  sized by the skin), `guideInset` (the step's card and Up next set in
  from the window's left edge), `guideUpRow` (each Up next row, with the
  step it shows) and `guideShows` (the tip in compact mode, finished goals
  kept in place, Up next without its heading, a goals area that never
  scrolls, the class quest card on the chain's first step only).
