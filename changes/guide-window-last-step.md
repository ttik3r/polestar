- The guide window can show the steps before the one on screen, one dimmed
  line each over the step, marked done or skipped: Settings > Guide window >
  Parts > Step card > Steps before (0 to 5, 0 by default: off). The oldest is
  on top, the newest right over the step. Click one to go back to that step.
  Compact mode leaves them out. The Atlas layout starts with one.
